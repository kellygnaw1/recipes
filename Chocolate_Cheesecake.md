# Chocolate Cheesecake

Rich chocolate cheesecake with a cocoa biscuit crust, chocolate ganache, raspberries, and white chocolate shavings.

**Pan:** 8-inch springform pan  
**Oven:** 325°F (163°C) for the cheesecake  
**Chill:** At least 6 hours, preferably overnight

## Ingredients

### Crust
- 200 g cocoa biscuits
- 80 g butter, melted

### Cream Cheese Filling
- 24 oz (680 g) cream cheese, softened
- 297 g granulated sugar
- 21 g cocoa powder
- 255 g 70% dark chocolate, melted and slightly cooled
- 4 large eggs, room temperature

### Chocolate Ganache
- 100 g regular chocolate chips
- 100 ml full-fat heavy cream
- 10 g butter

### Garnish
- White chocolate shavings
- Fresh raspberries

## Instructions

### 1. Make the Crust
1. Preheat the oven to **350°F (175°C)**.
2. Crush the cocoa biscuits into fine crumbs.
3. Mix with the **80 g melted butter** until evenly moistened.
4. Press firmly into the bottom of an **8-inch springform pan**.
5. Bake for **8–10 minutes**, then let cool. Reduce oven temperature to **325°F (163°C)**.

### 2. Make the Filling
1. Melt the **255 g dark chocolate** and set aside to cool slightly.
2. Beat the softened cream cheese and sugar together until smooth.
3. Mix in the cocoa powder.
4. Add the melted chocolate and mix until combined.
5. Add the eggs **one at a time**, mixing on low speed just until incorporated. Avoid overmixing.
6. Pour into the cooled crust.

### 3. Bake
1. Place the springform pan in a larger roasting pan. Protect the springform from leaks (for example, with a waterproof cake-pan barrier).
2. Pour hot water into the roasting pan until it reaches about **1–1½ inches** up the outside of the cheesecake pan.
3. Bake at **325°F (163°C)** for approximately **50–75 minutes**, checking the cheesecake rather than relying on the clock. Baking time depends on pan depth and your oven.
4. The cheesecake is ready when the edges are set but the center still has a gentle, Jell-O-like wobble.
5. Turn off the oven, crack the door, and let the cheesecake cool inside for **30–60 minutes**.
6. Remove from the water bath, cool to room temperature, then refrigerate for **at least 6 hours**, ideally overnight.

### 4. Make the Ganache
1. Heat the **100 ml heavy cream** until steaming, but not boiling.
2. Pour over the **100 g chocolate chips** and let sit for **2–3 minutes**.
3. Stir until smooth, then mix in the **10 g butter**.
4. Let the ganache cool slightly so it thickens but remains pourable.
5. Spread over the **fully chilled cheesecake** and refrigerate until set.

### 5. Garnish and Serve
1. Top with **fresh raspberries** and **white chocolate shavings**.
2. Slice with a warm, clean knife for neat edges.

## Notes
- Use **room-temperature cream cheese and eggs** for a smoother filling.
- Mix gently after adding eggs to minimize cracks.
- Keep the cheesecake refrigerated until serving.
