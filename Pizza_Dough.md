# Homemade Pizza Dough Recipe 🍕

**Yield:** 2 medium-thickness pizzas (11 × 9 inches each)  
**Flour:** Caputo Chef's Red 00  
**Hydration:** 67% (before any extra flour)  
**Cold fermentation:** 18–24 hours

## Ingredients

- 500 g Caputo Chef's 00 flour
- 336 g water (room temperature)
- 13 g salt
- 1 g instant dry yeast
- 16–20 g olive oil (for the pans)
- 6–10 g semolino (for the pans)

## Day 1 — Make the dough

1. **Mix:** Stir instant yeast into the water (no activation needed). Add flour, then salt, and mix until combined.
2. **Rest:** Cover for 20 minutes.
3. **Knead:** Knead for 8–10 minutes until smooth and elastic. If the dough feels too moist or sticky, add flour a little at a time until it has a soft, bouncy texture. Slight tackiness is normal; avoid adding too much flour.
4. **Stretch and fold (optional):** Rest 15 minutes, stretch and fold all four sides; rest another 15 minutes and repeat.
5. **Divide:** Split into 2 equal pieces (about 425 g each) and form dough balls.
6. **Refrigerate:** Put each ball into a lightly oiled, covered container. Refrigerate for 18–24 hours; no need to wait for the dough to double first.

## Day 2 — Bake

1. **Warm up:** Remove dough from the fridge 2–3 hours before baking.
2. **Prepare pans:** Oil two 11 × 9-inch aluminum pans and sprinkle lightly with semolino.
3. **Stretch:** Gently spread each dough ball into its pan. If it springs back, rest 15–20 minutes and try again.
4. **Proof:** Keep covered until visibly puffy.
5. **Preheat:** Heat the Miele oven to 475–500°F (245–260°C), or its highest suitable baking temperature.
6. **Bake:** Add tomato sauce and bake for about 7–10 minutes. Add mozzarella and other toppings, then bake for another 4–7 minutes, until the top and bottom are golden and the center is cooked through. Adjust time for your oven and pan.

## Tips

- Aim for dough that is **soft, elastic, bouncy, and slightly tacky**, not dry.
- Don't punch down the dough after refrigeration.
- Use only a little semolino to avoid a gritty bottom.
- Aluminum makes a lighter crisp crust; cast iron can make a richer, crispier bottom.
